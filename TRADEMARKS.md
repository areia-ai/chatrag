# The ChatRAG name and logo

The code is MIT-licensed. The name "ChatRAG" and the ChatRAG logo are not part
of that license.

**Fine without asking:**

- Saying your product is "built with ChatRAG" or "based on ChatRAG".
- Running ChatRAG, modified or not, for yourself, your company, or your
  clients, including with the default branding left in place.
- Writing about ChatRAG, teaching it, or linking to it.

**Needs a different name:**

- Publishing, distributing, or selling a fork, template, starter kit, or hosted
  service. Call it something that isn't "ChatRAG" and doesn't look like it, and
  swap out the logo in `public/`.
- Anything that suggests your version is the official one or is endorsed by
  the ChatRAG project.

If you're unsure, ask at hello@chatrag.ai.
